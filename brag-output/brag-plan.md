# Brag Plan: ROLLDEEP

## What is this app?
A free local study lab. You hit ROLL, get one syllabus topic plus one curiosity question plus a 25–60 minute window, study however you want, make a video so you actually did it. That topic never comes back.

## The angle
Study apps sell choice. ROLLDEEP sells the opposite: you don't pick the easy chapter. Free product for people who keep opening Notion instead of opening the book. The ROLL button is the product.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Cream paper. Grid. Archivo Black:
YOU DON'T HAVE A SYLLABUS PROBLEM.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Giant vermillion ROLL button slams in. A cursor clicks it. It presses (offset shadow collapses).
- Topic card lands: TOPIC Dijkstra's algorithm / QUESTION If a vertex is already marked done, what can a later negative edge do that Dijkstra has already forbidden itself from undoing? / ★★★★☆ / 50 MIN
- TAKE THIS bar. The word FREE in a yellow chip.

## Outro / punchline
ROLLDEEP
Free. Local. One topic. Never repeat.
github.com/dev-the-dev-while-deving/rolldeep-lab

## User flow worth showing
Open lab → smash ROLL → get topic + question + stars + time → TAKE THIS.

## Tone
- Preset: default
- Creative direction: free product that sells discipline — neo-brutalist study tool, not a dashboard
- Interpretation: Punchy type, hard cuts, hold long enough to read. The joke is the product is mean on purpose. No SaaS softness.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Share copy (draft)
You don't need another study app. You need a ROLL button. ROLLDEEP is free, local, and it will not let you pick the easy chapter.

## Audio direction
- Role: punchy upbeat bed with motion-matched UI hits
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3
- Music treatment: start ~0s, volume 0.32, fade last 1.2s under logo; punchy compact loop
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`, tempo ~110 BPM. Strong cues in window: 15.82, 18.01, 20.19. Lock outro slam to 18.01s. Sequential ROLL press + card land snap to 6.28 / 6.82 / 7.35 / 11.47.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; ROLL button shadow and yellow chip glow breathe with RMS. No waveform.
- SFX posture: moderate; dice/click for ROLL, card-place for topic card, bell for outro
- Audio-coupled moments: simulated click on ROLL; card landing; TAKE THIS slam
- Restraint rule: no glitch, no punch stacks, no every-beat SFX
